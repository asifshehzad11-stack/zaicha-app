ZAICHA · GUIDE

Zaicha integration

1 · The one rule

This orrery does not calculate a horoscope. It draws whatever your app’s zaicha says. Your app (its ephemeris) stays the single source of truth; the orrery only receives the finished positions. Every time a new zaicha is made — new date of birth, time or place — send it again and the planets, houses, symbols and terms move to exactly the places the zaicha chart shows. Two separate calculators would slowly disagree, so the orrery never computes on its own.

2 · Try it now

Paste a zaicha, press APPLY.

3 · Data your app sends

FIELDMEANING
titleShown above the chart, e.g. name + date of birth.
birthOptional record: {date:"1990-05-14", time:"06:30", tz:"+05:00", place:"Lahore"}. Only displayed and returned; never used for calculation.
zodiac"sidereal" or "tropical" — label only. The longitudes must already be in that zodiac.
houseMode"fixed" = house 1 is Aries (Lal Kitab). "ascendant" = whole-sign houses; needs ascendant (longitude 0–360). House 1 = the sign of the ascendant.
planetsKeys: sun moon mars mercury jupiter venus saturn rahu ketu. Each planet takes one of: {lon:200.5} (best, exact) · {sign:"libra", deg:20.5} · {house:10} (Lal Kitab — placed at the middle of the house). Ketu is optional (= Rahu + 180°).
housesAlternative to planets: {"10":["sun","jupiter"],"1":["moon"]}.

Longitudes: 0° = start of Aries, counted through 360°. Use the same longitudes your app uses to draw its chart (same ayanamsa, same node type — mean or true).

4 · Three ways to deliver it

  • URL (works for an artifact opened by link) — add #zaicha= + base64 of the JSON. A new link = a new zaicha. In JavaScript: page + "#zaicha=" + btoa(unescape(encodeURIComponent(JSON.stringify(z)))). Changing only the part after # updates the page without reloading.
  • iframe + postMessage (your app embeds the page):
    frame.contentWindow.postMessage({type:'orrery:setZaicha', zaicha:z}, '*');
    Listen for answers: orrery:zaicha (result, warnings, resulting houses), orrery:select, orrery:houseselect. Pin ORRERY_ALLOWED_ORIGINS in the script to your domain in production.
  • Same page (script): Orrery.setZaicha(z) → returns {ok, warnings, houses}.

5 · Example

{
  "title": "Asif · 14-05-1990 06:30 Lahore",
  "birth": {"date":"1990-05-14","time":"06:30","tz":"+05:00","place":"Lahore"},
  "zodiac": "sidereal",
  "houseMode": "ascendant",
  "ascendant": 100.4,
  "planets": {
    "sun":{"lon":29.8}, "moon":{"lon":195.2}, "mars":{"lon":101.0},
    "mercury":{"lon":12.5}, "jupiter":{"lon":75.3}, "venus":{"lon":40.1},
    "saturn":{"lon":301.7}, "rahu":{"lon":44.0}
  }
}

6 · What you can rely on

  • After APPLY the orrery re-reads every body’s position from its own 3D scene and reports the house it computed. If your data also carried a house that disagrees with the longitude, the status box shows a warning (it usually means a different house system or ayanamsa in the sending code).
  • Symbols, houses, the 2D chart and the astrological terms panel are all driven by that one placement, so they cannot disagree with each other.
  • A new zaicha pauses the simulation at the birth moment. Press PLAY to watch time move forward from it (the sky animation is illustrative, not an ephemeris).
  • Planet distances in the 3D scene are approximate. Angles seen from Earth (the zodiac position) are exact to the data sent, except when a planet’s stated elongation is impossible in the model (rare) — then the nearest reachable point is used and the longitude still matches.

7 · Checklist before trusting a chart

  1. Sidereal vs tropical — same as the app’s chart?
  2. Ayanamsa (e.g. Lahiri) already applied to the numbers?
  3. Mean or true Rahu — same as the app’s chart?
  4. Time zone and place converted to UT before your ephemeris ran?
  5. Compare one planet by hand: house in the orrery = house in the app.
